## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict

| Criteria | Techie Andrew (`techieandrew.vercel.app`) | RoughClick Digital (Ours) | Winner for Your Business |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Business Model** | **B2C Digital Store & Creator Portfolio:** Sells downloadable source code and project templates (₹60–₹199). | **B2B Digital Engineering Agency:** Delivers custom web development, web apps, and social media solutions for clients. | **RoughClick Digital** (Exact match for outsourcing business) |
| **Target Audience** | Students, aspiring developers, and hobbyists looking for cheap ready-made project files. | Business owners, founders, and companies looking to hire a professional agency. | **RoughClick Digital** (Attracts high-value contracts) |
| **Blog Management** | Public blog powered by third-party desktop Sanity CMS. | **Custom In-House Mobile Admin (`/admin-blog`):** Allows instant blogging directly from smartphones. | **RoughClick Digital** (Meets your exact mobile blogging requirement) |
| **Inquiry & Sales** | Generic email link + E-commerce Cart. | **High-Conversion B2B Funnel:** Direct WhatsApp chat, interactive quote builder, timeline selector, and office details. | **RoughClick Digital** (Built to convert business leads) |

> **Verdict:**  
> **RoughClick Digital is the superior and correct choice.** Techie Andrew is a digital product shop for an individual developer. Adopting that model would confuse business clients who are looking to outsource custom website and app development.

## 2. Detailed Dimension Comparison

### 2.1 Brand Identity & Positioning
* **Techie Andrew:**  
  * Focuses on solo developer persona (*"Night-mode enthusiast"*, *"Powered by coffee"*, lofi audio player).  
  * Suitable for: Indie hackers, YouTubers, and course creators.
* **RoughClick Digital (Ours):**  
  * Focuses on enterprise-grade reliability, clean hand-crafted code, and business performance (*"Sub-0.7s Core Web Vitals"*, *"100% Code & IP Ownership"*, *"Direct Builder Access"*).  
  * Suitable for: Service agencies winning corporate and SMB clients.

* **RoughClick Digital (Ours):** Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, Custom CSS Design System + Tailwind, Lucide Icons, In-House Mobile Admin CMS (Zero recurring SaaS fees), Interactive Capabilities Inspector, Theme Provider (Dark/Light).

## 3. Best Practices to Borrow from Techie Andrew

To make RoughClick Digital even stronger, we recommend adopting two key features from Techie Andrew:
1. **Clickable Live Demos:** Add direct "Live Preview" links to our portfolio items so potential clients can test live applications immediately.
2. **Transparent Starting Prices:** Add "Starting from ₹XX,XXX" indicators on the services page to give prospective clients immediate budget clarity.
